How it looks

Every session at a glance. One chat to steer them.

Verified, not claimed

Perzian runs your declared checks itself and records the real exit code. A claim is marked as a claim.

Your call, one keystroke

At a fork, an agent brings you the options and the tradeoff. Press 1, 2 or 3 and it moves on.

Parallel by default

Every agent works in its own worktree, so your checkout stays clean.

Never lose the thread

Sessions survive restarts. Bring your Claude Code history along.
